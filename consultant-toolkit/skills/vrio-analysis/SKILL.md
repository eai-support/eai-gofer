# VRIO Analysis

Judge which resources and capabilities give a lasting competitive advantage.

## Input

- A list of the firm's resources and capabilities, or enough context to draft one.

## Method

1. List 8 to 12 candidates: tangible (assets, locations), intangible (brand, IP, data), and organizational (processes, culture, relationships).
2. For each, answer with evidence:
   - Valuable: does it raise customer value or lower cost?
   - Rare: do few competitors have it?
   - Inimitable: is it costly or slow to copy or substitute?
   - Organized: is the firm set up to capture its value?
3. Classify the result:
   - Not valuable: competitive disadvantage.
   - Valuable, not rare: parity.
   - Valuable and rare, easy to copy: temporary advantage.
   - Valuable, rare, hard to copy, not organized: unused advantage.
   - All four: sustained advantage.
4. Recommend: protect, build, exploit, or stop investing.

## Output

1. Table: Resource or capability | V | R | I | O | Evidence | Result | Action.
2. Short list of sustained and unused advantages.

## Quality bar

- Each Yes or No has evidence, not opinion.
- Capabilities are specific, not "great people".

## Boundaries

- Not for activity costs: use `value-chain`.
- Not for the overall strategic summary: use `swot-analysis`.

## Rules

- If a required input is missing, ask once when you can. Otherwise state an assumption, label it [Assumption], and continue.
- Do not invent facts or figures. Label estimates [Estimate] and cite every source.
