# Stakeholder Map

Identify who can make or break the initiative, and plan how to engage each one.

## Input

- The initiative, the organization, and known people or groups affected.

## Method

1. List individuals and groups who are affected by, or can influence, the initiative.
2. Rate influence (High, Medium, Low) and interest (High, Medium, Low).
3. Rate current stance from -2 (opposed) to +2 (champion), and the stance you need.
4. Record each stakeholder's concerns and what they gain or lose.
5. Choose an approach by position: manage closely (high influence, high interest), keep satisfied, keep informed, or monitor.
6. Set actions: message, channel, messenger, frequency, and owner.

## Output

1. Register: Stakeholder | Influence | Interest | Current stance | Needed stance | Concerns | Approach | Actions | Owner.
2. Influence-interest grid positions.

## Quality bar

- Every high-influence stakeholder has a named owner and action.
- Gaps between current and needed stance are explicit.

## Boundaries

- Not for the audience of one deliverable: use `audience-brief`.
- Not for change interventions for whole groups: use `change-plan`.

## Rules

- If a required input is missing, ask once when you can. Otherwise state an assumption, label it [Assumption], and continue.
- Do not invent facts or figures. Label estimates [Estimate] and cite every source.
