# Growth Strategy

Find and prioritize where and how the business can grow.

## Input

- Current revenue mix, market data (from `market-sizing`), and strategic position (from `swot-analysis`).

## Method

1. Decompose recent growth: market momentum, share gain or loss, pricing, and M&A.
2. Generate avenues with the Ansoff matrix:
   - Market penetration: more share with current products in current markets.
   - Market development: current products in new markets or segments.
   - Product development: new products for current customers.
   - Diversification: new products in new markets.
3. For each avenue, choose a mode: build, partner, or buy.
4. Place avenues on three horizons: core (now), emerging (1-3 years), options (3+ years).
5. Score each avenue: size, right to win, time to impact, investment, and risk.
6. Rank into a long list and a short list.

## Output

1. Growth map table: Avenue | Ansoff type | Mode | Horizon | Size | Right to win | Time to impact | Investment | Risk | Score.
2. Short list with a one-line rationale each.

## Quality bar

- Each avenue has a size and a reason we can win.
- The list covers all four Ansoff cells, even if some are rejected.

## Boundaries

- Not for choosing among mutually exclusive alternatives: use `strategic-options`.
- Not for allocating resources across existing units: use `portfolio-matrix`.
